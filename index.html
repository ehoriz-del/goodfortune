About Good Fortune

Good Fortune is a simple global lucky-number generator inspired by some of the world's best-known lottery games.

Choose a country, select a game, and generate a fresh set of random numbers based on that lottery's number format.

Good Fortune does not sell lottery tickets and does not provide winning-number predictions. Every combination has the same chance of being drawn.
