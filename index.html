About Good Fortune

Good Fortune is a simple global lucky-number generator inspired by some of the world's best-known lottery games.

Choose a country, select a game, and generate a fresh set of random numbers based on that lottery's number format.

Good Fortune does not sell lottery tickets and does not provide winning-number predictions. Every combination has the same chance of being drawn.

Historical statistics are compiled from official public records where available and reputable third-party archives where necessary. Sources may correct, delay, restrict, or revise their data. Statistics are provided for general information and entertainment only and should not be treated as betting, financial, or predictive advice. Always confirm winning numbers, game rules, eligibility, deadlines, and prizes with the official operator.
